Business Review Game

Chapters 1–4

How to Play

  1. The group is divided into two teams.
  2. Each round, both teams get one 2-minute turn.
  3. During a turn, one team member looks at the computer. Their teammates should not be able to see the screen.
  4. Describe the large term without saying the three forbidden words shown underneath it. You are allowed to tell your team the category that your term is in. The category will be listed directly underneath the timer, and it will be one of these four categories: Business Basics, Understanding Economics, Globalization, or Ethics & CSR.
  5. If the team guesses correctly, select Correct to earn one point and receive another card.
  6. If the clue-giver says a forbidden word or wants to pass, select Skip. No point is awarded. Only one word can be skipped per turn. A team member from the opposite team should be watching the screen to make sure no forbidden words are said.
  7. When time expires, the turn immediately ends and play passes to the other team.
