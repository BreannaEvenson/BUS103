Growing & Expanding a Business

We've already looked at how a business can be owned. Now we'll look at ways businesses can grow and expand, including mergers, acquisitions, and franchising.

Merger vs. Acquisition

Merger

A merger happens when two companies combine to become one organization.

Acquisition

An acquisition happens when one company purchases another company and takes control of it.

Quick Reflection

Why might a company choose to merge with or acquire another company instead of growing completely on its own?

  • Gain customers: A company may instantly gain the other company's existing customers rather than spending years trying to attract them one by one.
  • Enter new markets: Merging with or buying another company can help a business move into a new city, region, or type of market more quickly than starting from scratch.
  • Acquire resources/technology: A company may want access to equipment, employees, patents, systems, or technology that another company already has.
  • Grow faster: Building growth internally can take a long time. A merger or acquisition may allow a company to expand much more quickly.

How Franchising Works

Franchisor

The franchisor is the established company or brand.

Provides:

  • Brand name
  • Business system
  • Training
  • Support
  • Marketing guidance
  • Operating standards

Franchisee

Cooperatives

A cooperative is a business that is owned and controlled by the people who use its services.

Instead of existing mainly to benefit outside investors, a cooperative is organized to serve its members. Cooperatives are common in areas such as agriculture, retail, and financial services.

Quick Summary

  • A merger is when two companies combine into one organization.
  • An acquisition is when one company purchases another company.
  • In franchising, the franchisor provides the brand and system, while the franchisee pays to operate under that system.
  • A royalty is an ongoing fee a franchisee pays to the franchisor.
  • A cooperative is owned and controlled by the people who use its services.
